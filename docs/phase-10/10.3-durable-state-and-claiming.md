## Claim protocol

1. Worker requests one claim for a workspace.
2. PostgreSQL locks one eligible row with `SKIP LOCKED`.
3. The row changes from `READY` or `RETRYING` to `CLAIMED`.
4. A unique claim token, worker id and lease expiry are persisted.
5. Heartbeats extend only a still-valid lease owned by the token.
6. Completion succeeds only while the same claim remains valid.
7. Expired claims return to `READY`, unless maximum attempts are exhausted, in which case they become `FAILED`.

## Safety properties

- Two workers cannot claim the same row in the same claim window.
- A stale worker cannot complete work after its lease has expired or been replaced.
- Every query is scoped by `workspaceId`.
- Poison steps stop after `maxAttempts`.
- Terminal states cannot transition back into execution.

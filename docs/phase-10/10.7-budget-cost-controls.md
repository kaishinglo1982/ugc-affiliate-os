# Phase 10.7 — Budget and Cost Controls

## Status

Complete. `PostgresBudgetGateService` is the atomic preflight/reserve/consume/release path for `BudgetPolicy` / `CostReservation`.

## Objective

Close the gap recorded in `10.1-existing-state-inventory.md`: "Budget enforcement | Policy/reservation data exists | Atomic preflight/reserve/consume/release service not proven | 10.7". Both models already carried every column needed (`limitAmount`, `period`, `alertThresholdPct`, `action`, reservation `status`, `expiresAt`); no migration was required.

## Delivered

- `reserve()`: workspace-and-policy-scoped, advisory-lock-serialized preflight check against the policy's current period (`daily`, `weekly`, or `monthly`) consumption (`SUM` over `RESERVED` + `CONSUMED` reservations in the window), then inserts a `RESERVED` reservation;
- policy `action` is honored as written: `block` policies deny (`BudgetExceededError`) once the projected total would exceed `limitAmount`; `alert` policies never block, but the result carries `overThreshold: true` once the projected total crosses `alertThresholdPct` of the limit — this is a **hard stop only for policies that declare themselves as hard stops**, not a silent override of the policy's own configuration;
- `consume()`: atomically transitions `RESERVED -> CONSUMED`, optionally reconciling to an actual cost different from the original estimate;
- `release()`: atomically transitions `RESERVED -> RELEASED`; both `consume` and `release` are single-use (a non-`RESERVED` reservation cannot be consumed or released again);
- `releaseExpired()`: sweeps `RESERVED` reservations past `expiresAt` to `EXPIRED`, matching the sweep pattern already used by 10.3's step-claim recovery and 10.4's capacity-reservation recovery;
- 11 integration tests: within-limit reserve, alert-threshold flagging without blocking, blocking denial, alert-policy over-limit allowance, unknown/disabled policy denial, unsupported period denial, concurrent reservations racing a blocking limit, consume with reconciliation and replay denial, release and its replay denial, not-found handling, and expiry sweeping.

## Reuse decision

The service extends the existing `BudgetPolicy` / `CostReservation` foundation (already used read-only by `src/app/api/budgets/route.ts` for policy CRUD). No second budget or cost-tracking model was introduced. Placed under `src/lib/finance/` per `docs/architecture/DOMAIN_OWNERSHIP.md`, which assigns "revenue, cost, tax estimates and financial reconciliation" to the `finance` domain (Finance Platform owner, Data Platform reviewer).

## Security rules

- Every query is scoped by `workspaceId`; a reservation or policy from one workspace is never visible to another.
- Deny by default: an unknown or disabled policy, an unsupported period, or a block-policy over-limit projection all refuse the reservation before any row is written.
- The per-(workspace, policy) advisory lock (`pg_advisory_xact_lock`) prevents two concurrent reservations from both passing the same limit check and jointly overspending it — verified under real concurrency in the integration test suite.

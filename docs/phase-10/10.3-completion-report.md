## Concurrency guarantees

1. A step is eligible only in `READY` or `RETRYING`.
2. PostgreSQL locks one candidate while skipping rows claimed by concurrent workers.
3. Claiming increments `attemptCount` and optimistic `version` in the same statement.
4. Heartbeat and completion require the exact workspace, step id and claim token.
5. Expired tokens cannot complete work after lease recovery.
6. Retry exhaustion moves the step to `FAILED`; poison work cannot loop indefinitely.
